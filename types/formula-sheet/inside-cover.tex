\bocsection{In the exam}
\begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{3pt}
\item Write the formula first, then put the numbers in: the formula alone often earns a mark.
\item Read the symbols in the Notation column before you substitute, and check the units.
\item Not sure where a formula comes from? Its Reference opens the derivation.
\ifcsname r@sec:key_formulas\endcsname\item The \hyperref[sec:key_formulas]{\covbf{Key formulas}} page at the end gathers the
	rows marked {\color{orange!80!black}$\bigstar$}.\fi
\ifcsname r@sec:symbol_index\endcsname\item Lost a symbol? The \hyperref[sec:symbol_index]{\covbf{Symbol index}} at the end
	links to the row where it first appears.\fi
\end{itemize}
